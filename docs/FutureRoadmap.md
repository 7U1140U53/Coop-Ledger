# Future Roadmap

**Project:** Coop Ledger  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-26

---

# 1. Purpose

This roadmap outlines the planned evolution of the Coop Ledger application. It serves as a planning document rather than a commitment to fixed delivery dates.

Development priorities will be guided by user feedback, project requirements, and technical considerations.

---

# Guiding Principles

Future development will focus on:

- Improving user experience
- Increasing security
- Enhancing reporting capabilities
- Supporting larger cooperative groups
- Maintaining a simple and maintainable architecture

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to evolve Coop Ledger into a comprehensive cooperative management platform capable of supporting:

- Cooperative savings
- Loans
- Investments
- Financial reporting
- Member management
- Administrative oversight

while maintaining a secure, scalable, and maintainable architecture.

---

# Summary

This roadmap provides a high-level view of the project's future direction. Features will be prioritized according to business needs, technical feasibility, and user feedback.
